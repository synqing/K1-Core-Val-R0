How it is reinstated (one Type-C island)

Placement, then wire, then labels. Never MCP-move a symbol. New parts get new coordinates.

  1. Place J1-PWR1 GT-USB-7005A / C5250872 with air. Origin proposal: (150, −4120) is fine if the retired part is gone. No second receptacle.
  2. Place D1-PWR1 USBLC6-2 at (520, −4120), RUSB_DP/DN 0 Ω immediately east, C1/C2 22 µF on the 5V_USB rail east of the B-column (x≥430), not on A-column pins.
  3. Keep RCC1/RCC2 5.1 kΩ Rd. DNP option resistors (RCC1S/B, RCC2S/B) stay DNP options, off the pin field.
  4. Wire only this map — SuperSpeed and SBU stay NC: A4/A9/B4/B9 → 5V_USB, A1/A12/B1/B12 + S1–S4 → GND, A6/B6 → USB_DP_J1 → D1 → USB_DP_PROT → RUSB_DP → USB_DP_UP, A7/B7 → USB_DN_J1 → D1 → USB_DN_PROT → RUSB_DN → USB_DM_UP, A5 → USB_CC1, B5 → USB_CC2. A-column escapes west of x=90. B-column east of x=400. No vertical on a pin column. No segment through D1 or U1.
  5. Do not rebuild the OCS picture frame. OCS1/OCS2 get local stubs at U20.8 / U20.12 and a net label.
  6. Do not restore T2’s 3V3-on-DN or XTALOUT-on-GND. That is repaired forward on U20, after this island is a drawing again.
