# Mission

K1-CORE-VAL-R0 exists to make the eventual production K1 architecture provably correct.

It maximises bring-up capability, component evaluation, architectural comparison, observability,
measurement access, subsystem isolation, reversible configuration, alternative part testing,
fault localisation and future experimental expansion.

PCB size, component count and prototype BOM cost are secondary constraints. The board is not
compacted merely to look commercially finished. Where additional east-west area materially
improves RF, SI, PI, EMI, thermal behaviour, routing, measurement access or experimental
flexibility, the board grows.

## What this board is not

- Not the production cost-optimised K1 mainboard.
- Not a continuation of the legacy `k1_core` geometry. No legacy copper is imported.
- Not a place to prove firmware. Firmware parity lives in the DualMCU repository.
